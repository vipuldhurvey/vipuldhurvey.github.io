Turning Real-World Discipline Into Clean, Responsive Code

My path into software development started with curiosity about how complex systems work and grew through deliberate, hands-on effort. With a Diploma in Electrical Engineering (2016) and six years of industrial experience as a Machine Operator at Kewal Kiran Clothing Ltd., I learned firsthand what it means to operate precision machinery, follow structured workflows, stay calm under production pressure, and diagnose equipment issues before they escalate.

Alongside my industrial work, I completed my Bachelor of Arts in Psychology (2021) — strengthening how I think about user behavior, clarity, and intuitive interaction — and pursued structured Full Stack Web Development training at Coding Ninjas.

Today, I channel that steady work ethic into building responsive interfaces, interactive JavaScript tools, full-stack coursework applications, and practical web customization across HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Java, WordPress, Shopify, and database fundamentals — backed by dozens of public repositories you can inspect directly on GitHub.
